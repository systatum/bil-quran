Systatum ("Systatum", "we", "us", or "our") develops and operates **bil-Quran** (the "App"), a Quran application designed to provide users with access to Quranic content and related features.

This Privacy Policy explains how bil-Quran handles information when you use the App. In summary: bil-Quran does not require an account or login, and Systatum does not store user data or user preferences on its servers. App preferences are stored locally on the user's device and remain under the user's control.

## 1. Information We Collect

**We do not collect or store personal information on our servers.**

bil-Quran does not require you to create an account or log in to use the App. We do not ask you to provide personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or other identifying information in order to use the App.

We also do not maintain a server-side profile of your settings or preferences.

## 2. User Preferences

bil-Quran may store your app settings and preferences locally on your device.

These preferences may include settings such as your preferred display configuration or other choices you make while using the App.

This information is stored **locally on your device**, rather than on Systatum's servers.

Systatum does not have access to these locally stored preferences and does not maintain a copy of them on its servers.

## 3. Downloading Your Preferences

bil-Quran may provide functionality that allows you to download or export your own preferences.

Any such downloaded file is generated for you and remains under your control. Systatum does not receive or store a copy of your exported preferences.

## 4. No Account Required

You can use bil-Quran without creating an account or logging in.

Because the App does not require an account, Systatum does not maintain an account-based record of your activity, preferences, or identity.

## 5. How Your Information Is Stored

Information stored by bil-Quran for your preferences is stored locally on your device.

Systatum's servers do not store your personal information, app preferences, or user settings.

Consequently, Systatum does not have knowledge of the individual settings and preferences stored locally by users.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties because we do not collect or store personal information on our servers.

Your locally stored preferences are not transmitted to Systatum by bil-Quran.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your preferences are stored locally on your device, you control their retention.

You may delete locally stored App data through the applicable controls provided by the App or your device's operating system.

Deleting the App or its local data may also remove locally stored preferences.

## 8. Children's Privacy

bil-Quran does not require users to provide personal information or create an account.

Because we do not collect personal information through the App, we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to bil-Quran or our data practices.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this Privacy Policy.
