Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

And of the women past child bearing, who hope not for marriage, it is no sin on them if they put off their (outer) garments, without displaying their ornaments; Yet (Note how far Islam wants woman to guard her modesty and chastity by remaining behind the veil.) if they restrain themselves (even from this) it is better for them; And God is All-Hearing, All Knowing.

This exception for the aged women makes the necessity of the veil for other women clearly incumbent on them supporting the order in verses 24:30 & 24:31, dealing with veil for women. The last clause commands veil even for the aged women. (A.P.).
