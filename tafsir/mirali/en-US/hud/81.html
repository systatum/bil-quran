Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

Said they: (The angels.) "O' Lot! Verily we are messengers from thy Lord! Never shall they reach thee! so depart thou with thy people while a part of the night remaineth, and let none of you turn back - but thy wife; Verily shall befall her what befalleth them; Verily their appointed time is the morning; Is not the morning nigh?"
