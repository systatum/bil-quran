Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

And they (Came forth.) shall come forth before God all together, then shall (Said.) say the weak unto those who were arrogant: "Verily we had been your followers, can ye therefore avert from us any part of the chastisement of God?" They would say: "If God hath guided us, we too would have surely guided you; it is the same to us (now) whether we implore (impatiently) or we are (Use patience.) patient, (now) there is not for us (any) way of escape."
