And he entered the city at a time when unvigilant were its people, and found he therein two men fighting: the (Lit. This.) one, of his own party; and the other (Lit. This.) of his enemies; And the one of his own party sought his help against the one who was of his enemies, and Moses smote him with his fist and (Killed him. Refer to verse 28:33.) passed (death) on him; Said (Moses): "This (The man who was killed.) is of the Satan's doing; verily, he is an enemy, an open misleader."

Moses found an Egyptian who was Pharaoh's cook, beating a poor helpless Israelite who sought help from Moses. Moses asked the Egyptian to leave away the poor Israelite and when he did not cease beating the poor man, Moses rendered a blow by his fist just to make him stay away from the Israelite but the Egyptian succumbed to the blow and fell dead.
