Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

And We have enjoined on man goodness unto his parents; Did bear him his mother with pain and she delivered him with pain; And bearing him and weaning him is thirty months (Refer to verse 2:233.) ; Until when he attaineth his maturity and reacheth forty years, sayeth he: "My Lord! arouse (Grant me.) me that I may give thanks for Thy bounties which Thou hath bestowed on me and my parents and that I may do good which pleaseth Thee and do good unto me in respect of my offspring; Verily I turn unto Thee (repentant)., and verily I am one of the Muslims (Who submit themselves to God.)"

The maximum period of breast feeding for a child is 2 years by which time the child is equipped by nature with the set of teeth enabling it to chew and to masticate the ordinary food and to be independent of the mother's milk. The mother also, is automatically disabled from feeding the child from her breast for with the teeth in the mouth of the child it hurts her to affect the breast feeding and the milk also ceases to flow1.

The minimum extent of pregnancy is six months and deducting these months there remain only 24 months in the period of 3 years mentioned in this verse.

The Holy Apostle Yahya (John) and Husayn, the third Holy Imam, the second grandson of the Holy Prophet, these two holy ones were born in six months.

Note the reasoning with which man is enjoined to regard, revere, honour and respect the parents. Ihsan is not what is done in return or for any good but initiative in doing good without expecting any return for it.
