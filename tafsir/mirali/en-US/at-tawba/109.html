Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

Is he therefore better who hath laid his foundation on fear (fear of God's disfavour.) of God and (His) goodwill (Pleasure.) , or he who layeth his foundation on the brink of a crumbling hollowed (hypocrites.) bank, so it crumbled down with him into the fire of Hell; and God guideth not people (who are) unjust.

The reference is to the Mosque at Quba, which was founded on piety and purity, to please God. This is the first Mosque which the Holy Prophet built for Islam and that was at Quba at about 3.5 miles from Madina. The Holy Prophet liked this mosque so very much that once a week he used to go there from Madina and offered his prayers in it. This is why it is said that prayer in this mosque at Quba is equal to one 'Umra, i.e., the lesser pilgrimage to the Holy Ka'ba at Mecca.
