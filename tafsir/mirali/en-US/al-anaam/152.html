Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

And approach ye not the property of an orphan save that be in the best manner till he attaineth his maturity; Give ye full measure and full weight in (perfect) justice; We task not a soul but according to his ability, and when ye speak be just; though it be against a kinsman; and fulfil ye God's covenant (This serves as a reminder as well as a warning to the Muslims particularly about the Last Covenant taken from them by God through His apostle the Holy Prophet on 18th Dhu 'l-Hijja 10 AH at 'Ghadir Khumm'.) ; This He enjoineth you with so that ye may be mindful (Refer to Verse 4:6, 17:34.) .

The warning against approaching the property of any orphan save in the best manner, is a provision for improving the interest of the orphan in the property, if it could be through any business or investment, in which case the result must be to the benefit of the orphans. There is a reference to the faithful and careful custody of an orphan's property in 4:6.
