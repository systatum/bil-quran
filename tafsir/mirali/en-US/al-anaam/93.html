Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

And who is more (Tyrant - Wrong doer. Refer to Verses 6:21; 6:144; 6:157; 11:18; 39:32.) unjust than he who forgeth a lie against God, or sayeth "It hath been revealed unto me" while nothing had been revealed unto him, and he who sayeth: "I can jet down the like of what God rath sent down;" and if thou hadst seen when the unjust shall re in the agonies of death and the angels shall spread forth their hands, saying: "Surrender (Give out.) ye your souls (Indicates that soul is an entity which is taken away at death.) ; today ye shall be recompensed with a disgracing chastisement for what ye spoke against God other than the truth and (because) ye used to behave with pride (Arrogance) against His signs."

As the false prophet Musailama. the arch liar, used to compose his own sentences and claimed it to have been revealed to him - Abdullah Ibn Abi Sarah was the half-brother of Uthman. He used to record in writing, the revelations that were received by the Holy Prophet. He used to make his own alterations in the words of the Verses for which he was turned out of Madina. He used to say if what Muhammad says is a matter revealed, he also can dictate similar matter which must be revelation. This Verse was revealed about the Jew Abdullah Ibn Abi Sarah who had reverted to infidelity.
