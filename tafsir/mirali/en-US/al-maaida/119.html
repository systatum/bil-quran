God will say (Lit. said.) : "This is the day when shall benefit the truthful ones; their truth for them shall be gardens beneath which rivers flow to abide therein for ever; God is well pleased with them and they are well pleased with God; This is the great achievement (Refer to Verse 85:11.) ."
