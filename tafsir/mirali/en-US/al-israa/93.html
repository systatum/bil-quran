Tafsir Mir Ahmad Ali

Or there be for thee a house of (Ornament.) gold, or ascend thou into the heaven; and never will we believe in thy ascending until thou causeth to be sent down unto us a book that we may read it. "Say: thou (O' Our Apostle Muhammad!)" "Glory be to my Lord; am I aught save a man (sent by God as) Apostle."

'Imam or a human apostle of God', the working of a miracle depends totally upon the Will of the Lord and not upon the fancy of the people to demand it as and when it wants.
