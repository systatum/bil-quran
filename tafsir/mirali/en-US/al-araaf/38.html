He will say: "Enter ye among the people who indeed have passed away before you, of jinn and men into the (Hell) fire; Every time a (new) (A fresh batch of the Sinners.) people entereth (it) it shall curse its sister (Fellows in faith- Companions.) (people); until they have all come together into it, the later of them shall say about the former of them; "O' Our Lord! These are they who led us astray, therefore give them a double chastisement of the fire;" He (God.) will say: "For everyone (of you) double, but ye know."
