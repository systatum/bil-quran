Tafsir Ibn Kathir

And (mention) when Isa, the son of Maryam, said, "O Children of Israel, indeed I am the messenger of Allah to you confirming what came before me of the Torah and bringing good tidings of a messenger to come after me, whose name is Aḥmad."1 But when he came to them with clear evidences, they said, "This is obvious magic."2

See 61:5

  1. 1Another name of Prophet Muḥammad SAW.
  2. 2i.e., fraud or deception.
