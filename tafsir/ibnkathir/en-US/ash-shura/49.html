Tafsir Ibn Kathir

To Allah belongs the dominion of the heavens and the earth; He creates what He wills. He gives to whom He wills female (children), and He gives to whom He wills males.

To Allah belongs the kingdom of the heavens and the earth. He creates what He wills. He bestows female upon whom He wills, and bestows male upon whom He wills (42:49). Or He bestows both males and females, and He renders barren whom He wills. Verily, He is the All-Knower and is Able (to do all things) (42:50).

Allah tells us that He is the Creator, Sovereign and Controller of the heavens and the earth. Whatever he wills happens, and whatever He does not will does not happen. He gives to whomsoever He wills and withholds from whomsoever he wills; none can withhold what He gives, and none can give what He withholds, and He creates whatever He wills.

يَهَبُ لِمَنْ يَشَاءُ إِنَاثًا

He bestows female upon whom He wills.

means, He gives them daughters only. Al-Baghawi said, "And among them (those who were given daughters only) was Lut (AS)."

وَيَهَبُ لِمَنْ يَشَاءُ الذُّكُورَ

and bestows male upon whom He wills.

means, He gives them sons only. Al-Baghawi said, "Like Ibrahim Al-Khalil (AS), who did not have any daughters."

أَوْ يُزَوِّجُهُمْ ذُكْرَانًا وَإِنَاثًا ۖ

Or He bestows both males and females,

means, He gives to whomsoever He wills both males and females, sons and daughters. Al-Baghawi said, "Like Muhammad SAW."

وَيَجْعَلُ مَنْ يَشَاءُ عَقِيمًا ۚ

and He renders barren whom He wills.

means, so that he has no children at all. Al-Baghawi said, "Like Yahya (AS) and Isa (AS)." So people are divided into four categories: some are given daughters, some are given sons, some are given both sons and daughters, and some are not given either sons or daughters, but they are rendered barren, with no offspring.

إِنَّهُ عَلِيمٌ

Verily, He is the All-Knower

means, He knows who deserves to be in which of these categories.

قَدِيرٌ

and is Able (to do all things).

means, to do whatever He wills and to differentiate between people in this manner. This issue is similar to that referred to in the Ayah where Allah says of Isa (AS):

وَلِنَجْعَلَهُ آيَةً لِلنَّاسِ

And (We wish) to appoint him as a sign to mankind (19:21)

: i.e., proof for them of His power, for He created people in four different ways. Adam (AS), was created from clay, from neither a male nor a female. Hawwa' (AS), was created from a male without a female. All other people, besides Isa (AS), were created from male and female, and this sign of Allah was completed with the creation of Isa bin Maryam (AS), who was created from a female without a male. Allah says:

وَلِنَجْعَلَهُ آيَةً لِلنَّاسِ

And (We wish) to appoint him as a sign to mankind (19:21)

This issue has to do with parents, whilst the previous issue has to do with children, and in each case there are four categories. Glory be to the All-Knower Who is Able to do all things.
