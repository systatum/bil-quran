Tafsir Ibn Kathir

So Musa returned to his people, angry and grieved.1 He said, "O my people, did your Lord not make you a good promise?2 Then, was the time (of its fulfillment) too long for you, or did you wish that wrath from your Lord descend upon you, so you broke your promise (of obedience) to me?"

See 20:83

  1. 1The meaning may also be "angry and enraged."
  2. 2That He would send down the Torah, containing guidance for you.
