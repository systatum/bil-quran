Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Nor do they spend an expenditure, small or large, or cross a valley but that it is registered for them that Allah may reward them for the best of what they were doing.

Neither do they spend any contribution – small or great – nor cross a valley, but is written to their credit that Allah may recompense them with the best of what they used to do (9:121).

Allah said next:

وَلَا يُنفِقُونَ

Neither do they spend

Which is in reference to the fighters in Allah's cause:

نَفَقَةً صَغِيرَةً وَلَا كَبِيرَةً

any contribution – small or great –

Which is with regards to its amount:

وَلَا يَقْطَعُونَ وَادِيًا

nor cross a valley

While marching towards the enemy:

إِلَّا كُتِبَ لَهُمْ

but is written to their credit

For these actions that they take (and which are under their control):

لِيَجْزِيَهُمُ اللَّهُ أَحْسَنَ مَا كَانُوا يَعْمَلُونَ

that Allah may recompense them with the best of what they used to do.

Certainly, the Leader of the faithful, Uthman bin 'Affan (RA), acquired a tremendous share of the virtues mentioned in this honorable Ayah. He spent large amounts and tremendous wealth on this battle (Tabuk).

Abdullah, the son of Imam Ahmad recorded that 'Abdur-Rahman bin Khabbab As-Sulami said; The Messenger of Allah SAW gave a speech in which he encouraged spending on the army of distress (for Tabuk). Uthman bin 'Affan (RA)said; 'I will give one hundred camels with their saddles and supplies.' Then he SAW exhorted them some more. So Uthman said; 'I will give one hundred more camels with their saddles and supplies.' Then SAW he descended one step of the Minbar and exhorted them some more. So Uthman bin 'Affan said; 'I will give one hundred more camels with their saddles and supplies.' Then I saw Allah's Messenger SAW with his hand moving like this - and 'Abdus-Samad's (one of the narrators) hand went out like one in amazement - he SAW said:

مَا عَلَى عُثْمَانَ مَا عَمِلَ بَعْدَ هَذَا

It does not matter what Uthman does after.

It is also recorded in the Musnad that 'Abdur-Rahman bin Samurah said, Uthman brought a thousand Dinars in his garment so that the Prophet SAW could prepare supplies for the army of distress. Uthman poured the money on the Prophet's lap, and the Prophet SAW started turning it around with his hand and declaring repeatedly:

مَا ضَرَّ ابْنَ عَفَّانٍ مَا عَمِلَ بَعْدَ الْيَوْمِ

The son of 'Affān (i.e., 'Uthmān) will never be harmed by anything he does after today.

Qatadah commented on Allah's statement:

وَلَا يَقْطَعُونَ وَادِيًا إِلَّا كُتِبَ لَهُمْ

nor cross a valley, but is written to their credit

"The farther any people march forth away from their families in the cause of Allah, the nearer they will be to Allah."
