Tafsir Ibn Kathir

Exalted1 is He who took His Servant (i.e., Prophet Muḥammad SAW) by night from al-Masjid al-Ḥarām to al-Masjid al-Aqṣā,2 whose surroundings We have blessed, to show him of Our signs. Indeed, He is the Hearing,3 the Seeing.4

  1. 1Above any imperfection or failure to do as He wills.
  2. 2In Jerusalem.
  3. 3Who hears every sound, distinguishes every voice, understands every word, and accepts and responds to supplications.
  4. 4Who sees and understands all things apparent and unapparent, visible and invisible.
