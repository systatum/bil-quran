Tafsir Ali Quli Qara'i

Had We made it a non-Arabic1 Qur'ān, they2 would have surely said, 'Why have not its signs been articulated?' 'What! A non-Arabian [scripture]3 and an Arabian [prophet]!?' Say, 'For those who have faith, it is a guidance and healing; but as for those who are faithless, there is a deafness in their ears and it is lost to their sight.' They are [as if they were] called from a distant place.

  1. 1Or 'a barbaric Qur'ān;' that is, in a language other than articulate literary Arabic.
  2. 2That is, the Arabs.
  3. 3Or 'a barbaric scripture.'
